About Us

The story and the goals behind Find Your Lost Items.

Founder & Owner

Student BCA 1B · Basic Web Development project

Who we are

Find Your Lost Items is a lost-and-found website made by students, for students. It started as a Basic Web Development project, built by a team of two students with HTML5, CSS3, Bootstrap and JavaScript.

The founder designs, builds, and looks after the website, and reads every message sent through the Contact Us page. Our goal is simple: when something is lost on campus it should be easy to find it again, and the people who return things should be thanked.

Why this website exists

The problem

Lost items are often announced in busy chat groups where the message is buried within hours. People who find a wallet, a phone, or an ID card often do not know who the owner is.

Our focus: found items

Other places already collect reports of lost items. We also care about the other side: the found items and the honest students who bring them back.

A diverse campus

Our students come from many Indian states and from other countries. Every account shows where the student is from, to celebrate this diversity.

Our vision

A campus where every lost item finds its way back to its owner, and where honesty is noticed and celebrated.

Our mission

  • Make it easy. Report a lost or found item in about a minute, with a photo, a short description, and the place.
  • Reward honesty. Finders earn points, badges, and a place on the Finder Heroes board, but only after the owner confirms the return.
  • Protect people. Only logged in students can see the contact button, and a hidden detail helps the finder check the real owner.
  • Keep it simple and free. One website, no complicated steps, no cost for students.

What we value

Honesty

Returning what is not ours is the right thing to do, and we say thank you.

Community

Students help students. Every report can make someone’s day better.

Simplicity

Clear pages and few steps, so everyone can use the website from a phone.

Lost or found something?

Built with HTML5, CSS3, Bootstrap, JavaScript and Three.js. This is a front-end project, so the data stays in your own browser.
