Finder Heroes

Thank you to every student who returned a lost item to its owner.

Points chart

One bar for each finder. Height = points / 10. Gold, silver and bronze are the first three places.

    How to become a hero

    1. Found something? Report it with "I found an item". Or open a lost report and press "I found this item".
    2. The owner responds or contacts you, and you give the item back.
    3. The owner opens the item, presses "I received my item", and may leave feedback.
    4. Only after that the status becomes Returned, and you get 10 points and a badge.
    Good Samaritan: 1 return Campus Helper: 2 returns Campus Hero: 3 or more
